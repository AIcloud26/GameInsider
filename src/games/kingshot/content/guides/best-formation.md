In Kingshot, where you place heroes determines who takes damage first and how effectively skills chain. The fundamental rule is to protect your damage dealers while letting your front line absorb the opening pressure.

## The basic two-line principle

- **Front line:** place durable **Infantry** and tanky heroes here. Their job is to survive and hold the enemy's attention.
- **Back line:** place **Archers**, damage dealers and any healers or supports here, where they can output damage safely.

Cavalry heroes vary by kit — some work on the front, while others are better used as flanking damage, so read each hero's role before slotting them.

## Build a balanced squad

A reliable early formation includes:

1. **One or two durable front-liners** to absorb damage.
2. **One AoE damage dealer** to clear groups of enemies.
3. **One single-target or burst hero** for high-priority targets.
4. **Support or healing** if your roster provides it.

## Adjust to the mode

- **Defensive / tower-defence battles:** favour durable front liners and crowd control to stall waves.
- **Arena-style fights:** AoE and control heroes tend to shine against multiple targets.
- **Single-boss content:** lean into sustained single-target damage and heroes that perform well below half health.

## Counters and synergy

Pair heroes whose skills complement each other — for example, a defence-shredding support with a high-damage carry. Stuns and other control effects are especially valuable for interrupting dangerous enemy skills.
