Kingshot is a medieval strategy game from **Century Games** (the studio behind Whiteout Survival). It blends three familiar loops: **4X base building**, **tower-defence combat** and **idle resource generation**. You play a governor rebuilding after a rebellion — gathering resources, upgrading your town, recruiting heroes and defending against waves of enemies.

## Understand the core loop

Your early game revolves around a simple cycle:

1. **Upgrade your town center and resource buildings** (farms, mines and similar hubs).
2. **Recruit and level heroes**, who lead your troops in defence and campaigns.
3. **Defend the town** in tower-defence-style encounters.
4. **Complete daily missions and quests** for steady resources and speed-ups.

Since the game includes idle generation, resources keep accumulating while you are away — log in regularly to collect before your storage caps.

## Priorities for the first days

- **Follow the tutorial and chapter/quest tasks.** They guide the most efficient building order and unlock core systems.
- **Keep builders busy.** Avoid letting upgrade timers sit idle; queue shorter upgrades before logging off.
- **Upgrade resource and storage capacity together.** Raising production without storage wastes idle gains.
- **Invest in a few heroes, not everyone.** Focus limited experience and materials on your strongest early heroes (see our [best heroes guide](/games/kingshot/guides/best-heroes/)).
- **Join an alliance early** once the feature unlocks for help, speed-ups and group content.

## Common beginner mistakes

- Spending premium currency on rushed timers instead of high-value bundles.
- Levelling too many heroes evenly, which spreads scarce upgrade materials thin.
- Neglecting defence upgrades and losing resources to enemy waves.
- Letting resources hit the cap before spending them.
