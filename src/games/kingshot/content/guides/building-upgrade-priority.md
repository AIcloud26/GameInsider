# Kingshot Building Upgrade Priority Guide: What to Upgrade First

Building upgrades shape almost every part of your progress in Kingshot. The right choices help you unlock new levels, keep research moving, improve your army, and avoid spending resources on buildings that do not solve your current problem.

The mistake many players make is treating every building as equally important. A better approach is to identify what is blocking your next goal, upgrade the building that removes that obstacle, and then reassess your priorities.

This guide explains how to make those decisions without relying on a rigid upgrade order that may not fit every account.

## Quick Answer: What Should You Upgrade First?

For most developing accounts, use this decision order:

1. **Town Center and its required buildings:** Check what the next Town Center upgrade actually requires.
2. **Embassy and other prerequisites:** Upgrade them when they block Town Center progression or support an immediate alliance goal.
3. **Academy and research:** Keep long-term development moving instead of focusing only on visible building levels.
4. **Barracks, Range, and Stable:** Upgrade the troop buildings that support your current training and progression needs.
5. **Infirmary and Command Center:** Invest according to your combat exposure, troop capacity, rally activity, and current requirements.
6. **Resource and other support buildings:** Upgrade them when they solve a real shortage, unlock something important, or are required for progression.

This is a decision framework, not a universal level-by-level build order. Your in-game upgrade panel should determine the exact prerequisites and costs for your account.

## Building Upgrade Priority at a Glance

| Building | General priority | When to focus on it |
|---|---|---|
| Town Center | Very high | When progressing toward the next level or unlocking new capabilities |
| Required prerequisite buildings | Very high | When they block the next Town Center upgrade |
| Embassy | High when relevant | When required for progression or when alliance support matters |
| Academy | High | When research progression is a bottleneck |
| Barracks, Range, and Stable | Goal-dependent | When troop development or a required upgrade calls for them |
| Infirmary | Situation-dependent | Before demanding combat periods or when capacity is a concern |
| Command Center | Situation-dependent | When march or rally capacity matters to your activities |
| Resource buildings | Usually situational | When production, a prerequisite, or a specific shortage justifies the investment |
| Other support buildings | Requirement-dependent | When they unlock a useful feature or solve a current problem |

The most important distinction is between a building that blocks your next major upgrade and a building that is simply available to upgrade. Those are not always the same thing.

## 1. Town Center: Build Around Your Next Milestone

The Town Center is the starting point for your construction plan because its level affects overall building progression and unlock access.

Rather than upgrading random buildings and checking the Town Center later, reverse the process.

### Before starting a construction queue

- Open the Town Center upgrade panel.
- Record every listed prerequisite.
- Check the resource and time requirements.
- Identify which prerequisite is furthest behind.
- Decide whether another upgrade would help your immediate goal more.
- Keep the next Town Center upgrade in your plan while completing its requirements.

For example, if the next Town Center level requires an Embassy upgrade and a troop building, those buildings become immediate priorities. Upgrading an unrelated production building first may delay your main objective without providing a comparable benefit.

Do not rely on a fixed online prerequisite table without checking it in game. Requirements can change with progression systems and updates.

### Keep the whole queue productive

A long Town Center timer does not mean your account has to stop progressing. Use the waiting period to advance research, train troops, gather resources, and prepare the next prerequisite.

The aim is not to finish every building immediately. It is to make useful progress while your main construction runs.

## 2. Embassy: Upgrade for Progression and Alliance Value

The Embassy deserves attention when it appears in your Town Center requirements or when your alliance activity makes its benefits relevant.

Before spending resources, ask two questions:

- Is the Embassy directly blocking an important upgrade?
- Will improving it help with the alliance support and coordination you actually use?

An active alliance can make construction and research planning more efficient through available assistance systems. The practical value depends on your alliance's activity and the mechanics currently available in your game.

Do not upgrade the Embassy solely because a guide places it near the top of a ranking. Confirm its next benefit and the requirements of your account.

## 3. Academy: Protect Your Long-Term Progress

Building progression is only one part of account development. Research can support economic development, construction efficiency, troop performance, and other long-term goals, depending on the research options available to your account.

An Academy that falls behind can become a development bottleneck even when your visible building levels look impressive.

### A practical research routine

1. Check which research options are currently available.
2. Identify research that supports your immediate progression goal.
3. Compare its cost and duration with your available resources.
4. Start useful research when the queue is available.
5. Review the next research milestone while construction is running.

Avoid investing in research simply because it offers a large power number. Consider the benefit you will actually use and whether another research option better supports your account.

For broader early-game planning, see the [Kingshot Beginner Guide](/games/kingshot/guides/beginner-guide/).

## 4. Barracks, Range, and Stable: Upgrade With a Purpose

Troop buildings support different parts of your army development. Their value depends on which troops you train, which upgrades are required, and what your current combat goals demand.

Do not assume that all three buildings must always receive equal investment.

Instead, consider the following questions:

- Which troop building is required for your next Town Center milestone?
- Which troop types are important to your current army?
- Are you trying to unlock a new training tier or improve existing troop development?
- Would the resources spent here delay a more important prerequisite?
- Is your research keeping pace with your troop progression?

A building upgrade is useful when it creates a meaningful unlock, improves a relevant capability, or removes a progression bottleneck.

If a particular troop building is not needed for your next milestone, it may be reasonable to postpone an optional upgrade. However, do not neglect a required building simply because another one appears more attractive.

## 5. Infirmary: Consider the Cost of Losing Troops

The Infirmary should be evaluated in the context of your combat activity and available capacity.

If you regularly participate in demanding battles, an insufficient capacity can create a costly problem. If your current activity exposes your army to little risk, another building or research upgrade may offer greater immediate value.

Before a major combat period:

- Review your available Infirmary capacity.
- Consider how many troops you expect to expose to combat.
- Check whether healing resources and recovery time fit your plan.
- Avoid unnecessary battles when your recovery capacity is already under pressure.
- Upgrade when additional capacity addresses a real risk.

The Infirmary is not automatically the next building for every account. Its priority rises when troop preservation becomes a meaningful concern or when it is required for another upgrade.

Exact capacity, casualty rules, and upgrade requirements should be confirmed in the current game.

## 6. Command Center: Match Investment to Your Activities

The Command Center is particularly relevant to players who care about troop deployment and alliance rally activity.

Its value depends on how you participate in the game. A player who regularly organizes rallies may benefit from its relevant capacity improvements more directly than a player who rarely engages in those activities.

Consider upgrading it when:

- It is a prerequisite for your next major upgrade.
- Its capacity is limiting your normal deployment plans.
- You regularly organize or join activities where its benefits matter.
- The expected improvement justifies the resources and construction time.

If none of these conditions applies, it may be reasonable to focus on a more immediate bottleneck first.

The correct choice depends on the capabilities shown by your current Command Center and the requirements displayed in game.

## 7. Resource Buildings: Upgrade Only When the Numbers Make Sense

Resource production matters, but it should be evaluated against your actual resource needs.

A building that generates additional resources may help over time. However, its benefit depends on the amount produced, the time you will use it, your gathering activity, and the cost of the upgrade itself.

Before upgrading a resource building, ask:

- Which resource am I consistently short of?
- How much additional production will this upgrade provide?
- How long will it take for that additional production to become useful?
- Could gathering or another available activity solve the shortage more efficiently?
- Is this building required for my next progression milestone?

If the answer does not justify the investment, postpone the upgrade.

This does not mean resource buildings are useless. It means their priority should reflect the resource problem you are trying to solve rather than a desire to keep every building at the same level.

## 8. Early, Mid, and Late Progression

The best upgrade decision changes as your account develops.

### Early game: Unlock useful systems

Focus on Town Center progression and the prerequisites that unlock important features. Avoid spending heavily on optional upgrades before understanding which systems are available and which milestones matter to you.

Use early construction to build a clear progression path rather than attempting to perfect every part of the city.

### Mid-game: Balance progression and development

As construction and research become more demanding, plan several steps ahead.

Prioritize the next Town Center requirements while maintaining useful research and troop development. Consider alliance activity and troop protection when they become relevant to your regular gameplay.

At this stage, the most effective plan is often a cycle: identify the next requirement, complete it, start the main upgrade, and use the waiting period productively.

### Late game: Plan expensive milestones

As upgrades become more resource-intensive, mistakes can become more expensive.

Check the full prerequisite chain before committing rare materials or speedups. Compare the benefit of each upgrade against your longer-term goal, and avoid starting optional projects that prevent you from completing an important milestone.

Where advanced progression systems or special materials apply, use the current in-game requirements rather than assuming older guides still match the latest version.

## 9. A Practical Upgrade Checklist

Use this checklist whenever a building upgrade finishes.

- [ ] Check the next Town Center requirement.
- [ ] Identify the building that currently blocks progression.
- [ ] Confirm the exact cost and construction time in game.
- [ ] Check whether research can continue productively.
- [ ] Review troop-training needs and relevant building requirements.
- [ ] Assess Infirmary capacity before demanding combat activity.
- [ ] Consider Command Center improvements if deployment or rally capacity matters.
- [ ] Upgrade resource buildings only when the expected benefit is worthwhile.
- [ ] Preserve scarce resources for important upcoming milestones.
- [ ] Reassess the plan when a major upgrade completes.

The checklist is intentionally flexible. A player preparing for combat may prioritize troop protection differently from a player focused on unlocking a new Town Center level.

## 10. Common Building Upgrade Mistakes

### Upgrading everything evenly

Equal building levels may look organized, but they do not necessarily improve progression efficiently. Prioritize requirements and useful benefits instead.

### Ignoring the next prerequisite

A Town Center upgrade can be delayed by a building that was neglected several steps earlier. Check requirements before committing resources elsewhere.

### Focusing only on visible power

A higher power number does not automatically mean better long-term development. Consider research, unlocks, capacity, and the actual goal of the upgrade.

### Spending scarce resources on optional upgrades

Before starting an optional project, compare its benefits with the cost of delaying a required building.

### Following outdated level tables

Online tables can be useful references, but they should not replace the current in-game upgrade panel. Verify exact requirements before using rare resources or speedups.

### Forgetting to plan during long construction timers

Use waiting periods for research, troop training, gathering, and preparation. A long construction timer should not become a reason to leave every other useful activity idle.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the best building upgrade order in Kingshot?

Start with the Town Center and the buildings required for its next upgrade. Then prioritize research, troop development, alliance utility, and protection according to your current needs. The exact order depends on your account and the requirements displayed in game.

### Should I upgrade all buildings before upgrading the Town Center?

Not automatically. Focus on the buildings required for the next Town Center milestone and postpone optional upgrades when their benefits do not justify the delay.

### Is the Embassy always a top priority?

It is important when it blocks Town Center progression or provides alliance benefits you regularly use. Its immediate priority depends on your requirements and gameplay.

### Should I prioritize the Academy or troop buildings?

Check your immediate bottleneck. Research can support long-term development, while troop buildings matter for training and progression requirements. Neither should be prioritized blindly without considering your current goal.

### When should I upgrade the Infirmary?

Consider upgrading it when capacity is becoming a concern, before demanding combat periods, or when it is a required building. Evaluate its benefit against other immediate priorities.

### Should free-to-play players upgrade differently?

The same progression principles apply, but limited resources make selective investment more important. Focus on required buildings, useful research, and upgrades that provide a clear benefit before spending on optional improvements.

## Final Recommendation

The most reliable Kingshot building strategy is to upgrade with a goal rather than follow a fixed ranking.

Start with your next Town Center milestone, complete its actual prerequisites, and use construction downtime to advance research, troop development, and resource preparation. Invest in the Embassy, Infirmary, Command Center, and production buildings when their benefits match your current situation.

Before committing scarce resources, verify the requirements in game. A flexible plan based on your next bottleneck is more useful than upgrading every building simply because it is available.
