> **Status: Pending verification.** Hero strength changes with every balance patch and depends on game mode (Conquest, Expedition, Bear Hunt and Arena). Treat the names below as a starting point from multiple public rankings, not a fixed rule.

Kingshot heroes are divided into three broad classes — **Infantry, Archer and Cavalry** — and across different "generations." Newer-generation heroes generally have stronger base kits, but role and synergy matter more than generation alone.

## How to prioritise heroes

1. **Cover the core roles first:** a durable front line, area-of-effect damage and some support or healing.
2. **Build around the modes you play most.** A hero that dominates 5v5 Arena may be weak in Bear Hunt.
3. **Invest in a small core team** before spreading upgrade materials to the bench.

## Frequently high-rated heroes

Across several public rankings, the following names appear near the top (sources disagree on exact order):

- **Vivian (Archer)** — strong crowd control and area-of-effect damage; frequently cited as a top Arena and Expedition pick.
- **Triton (Infantry)** — defensive utility and sustain make him a strong front-line choice.
- **Jabel (Cavalry)** — obtained relatively early; provides AoE damage and team buffs.
- **Marlin (Archer)** — long-relevant AoE damage with stun utility.
- **Amadeus / Helga (Infantry)** — older but capable picks, especially when invested.

For the full ranked breakdown, see our [Kingshot tier list](/games/kingshot/tier-list/). Remember to **check the "updated" date**, because the meta shifts regularly.

## Free-to-play note

Some of the strongest heroes are higher-generation and hard to obtain without spending. If you are free-to-play, maximise your best available heroes and use synergy and positioning to compensate — our [formation guide](/games/kingshot/guides/best-formation/) explains how.
