# Kingshot Heroes List: Classes, Roles, and Upgrade Basics

Heroes are an important part of Kingshot progression. They influence how you approach battles, which team compositions are available to you, and where you may want to invest limited upgrade materials.

A useful hero reference should help you understand what to check before making a decision. Because the roster and balance can change, this page focuses on evaluating heroes rather than claiming to be a complete, permanently current list of every character.

## 1. Understand the hero classes

Kingshot hero references commonly organise heroes into classes such as Infantry, Archer, and Cavalry. Class labels are a useful starting point, but they should not replace reading each hero's actual skill descriptions.

### Infantry

When evaluating an Infantry hero, review its defensive capabilities, damage contribution, and any effects that help the team withstand an encounter. Do not assume every Infantry hero is purely a tank.

### Archer

When evaluating an Archer hero, examine its damage skills, target behaviour, and any additional effects listed in its abilities. The value of those effects depends on the encounter and the hero's development.

### Cavalry

Cavalry heroes can have different purposes depending on their individual kits. Review their skills and the role they can fill in your available formation rather than assigning every hero in the class the same job.

These are broad evaluation categories, not a verified skill-by-skill description of every hero in the current roster. Confirm class labels and abilities in the game.

## 2. Understand generations and rarity

Hero generation and rarity can influence how a hero is obtained and developed, but neither label alone tells you whether an investment is right for your account.

Before choosing a hero, ask:

- Can you obtain the hero consistently with your current play style?
- What materials are required to improve its level, stars, or skills?
- Does the hero offer something your current formation lacks?
- Can you develop the hero enough to make the investment worthwhile?
- Does the recommendation apply to the current version of the game?

New releases and balance changes can affect recommendations. Avoid treating a generation label as proof that a hero is automatically better for every mode or account.

## 3. What to check before upgrading a hero

The game may offer multiple ways to develop heroes. Check the current interface and item descriptions for the exact systems, costs, and requirements available to your account.

**Levels and base development:** Review the expected benefits and costs before committing experience or other materials.

**Stars, fragments, or hero-specific items:** Confirm which items are required, how they are obtained, and whether they are difficult to replace.

**Skills:** Read the actual skill descriptions and identify which effects change with further investment. Do not rely on an abbreviated community description if the in-game text differs.

**Equipment or additional progression systems:** If these features are available for the hero, compare their costs with other improvements you could make. Unlock requirements and systems may change over time.

A practical upgrade is one that improves a hero you use or helps address a specific weakness. The most expensive option is not necessarily the most useful one.

## 4. Compare heroes by purpose

When two heroes are competing for your resources, compare them using the same criteria.

| Evaluation point | What to check |
|---|---|
| Role | What does the hero contribute to the team? |
| Skills | Which effects are described in the current game? |
| Availability | Can you obtain and develop the hero reliably? |
| Investment | What resources are needed for useful progression? |
| Team fit | Does the hero complement your existing formation? |
| Activity | Is the hero relevant to the content you play? |

This comparison is more useful than looking at one headline ranking without considering your roster. Keep notes about your own results when practical, especially if you are deciding between two costly upgrades.

## 5. Find the right hero for your account

If you are new to Kingshot, start with heroes you already own and use regularly. Build a manageable core roster and expand it when you encounter a specific requirement that your current team cannot handle.

## 6. How to keep a hero reference useful

Hero information can become outdated when the game introduces new characters or changes existing abilities. Before acting on a guide, check the following:

- The article's last-updated date.
- Whether the listed hero and skills appear in your current game.
- Whether the advice refers to the activity you want to play.
- Whether the source explains its version, testing conditions, or ranking criteria.
- Whether a recommendation assumes investment levels you do not have.

When reliable evidence is unavailable, it is better to label a detail as unverified than to publish a confident but potentially incorrect claim.
