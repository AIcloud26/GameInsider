Heroes are the units that lead your armies and determine your power in Kingshot's battles. Recruiting the right heroes and investing materials wisely is one of the biggest progression decisions in the game.

## Hero classes

- **Infantry** — durable front-line heroes, often tanks and melee damage dealers. Many of the strongest defensive units are infantry.
- **Archer** — ranged damage dealers, frequently providing area-of-effect damage and control.
- **Cavalry** — mobile heroes with varied kits, from bruisers to flanking damage and debuffers.

## Generations and rarity

Kingshot releases heroes in **generations** (Gen 1, Gen 2 and so on), with newer generations generally offering stronger stats and skills. Rarer heroes are harder and slower to obtain and upgrade, so rarity and generation should both factor into your investment plan.

## How hero progression works

Heroes grow through the same general levers found in Century Games' other titles:

- **Levelling** to raise base stats.
- **Rarity/star upgrades** using hero-specific fragments or shards.
- **Skills and exclusive equipment** that unlock or enhance abilities at higher investment.

Exact material names and costs should be confirmed in game, as these can change between updates.
