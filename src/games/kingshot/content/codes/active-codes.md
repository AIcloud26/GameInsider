## How to redeem

**In game:**

1. Complete the tutorial and tap your **profile/avatar** in the top-left corner.
2. Open **Settings**.
3. Tap **Gift Code**.
4. Enter the working code and tap **Redeem** to collect your rewards.

**On the web:** if the in-game option is not available, Kingshot also provides an official redemption page referenced in community guides (the Century Games gift-code portal). Enter your account details and the code there.

## Why a code may fail

- **Case-sensitive:** enter letters and numbers exactly as shown — copy and paste is safest.
- **Expired:** many codes last only days.
- **Usage limit:** some codes stop after a number of redemptions.
- **Region or account restrictions** may occasionally apply.
