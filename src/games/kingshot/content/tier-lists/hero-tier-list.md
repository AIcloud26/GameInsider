> **Status: Pending verification.** Public rankings disagree with one another and change each patch. The grouping below is a synthesis of several sources for orientation; it is not a definitive competitive ranking for every mode.

This tier list ranks Kingshot heroes by **overall value across the game's modes** (Conquest, Expedition, Bear Hunt and Arena). Mode-specific rankings can differ significantly, so read the notes before investing rare materials.

## What the tiers mean

- **S+** — top performers across multiple modes; prioritise these.
- **S** — very strong and reliable in most lineups.
- **A** — strong mid-game or mode-specific choices.
- **B and below** — situational or outclassed by newer heroes.

## Overall ranking (synthesis)

| Tier | Heroes |
| --- | --- |
| **S+** | Vivian, Triton, Alcar, Jabel |
| **S** | Thrud, Sophia, Yang, Marlin, Hilde, Margot |
| **A** | Eric, Rosa, Long Fei, Howard, Quinn, Jaeger, Petra, Zoe, Amadeus, Helga |

Names appear in different tiers depending on the source — for example, some rank Amadeus and Helga much higher when fully upgraded, while others place them as budget options. **Generation matters:** newer heroes (Gen 5/6) generally outperform older ones on raw stats.

## Mode guidance

- **Arena (5v5):** Vivian and other AoE/control heroes tend to dominate.
- **Bear Hunt / single boss:** sustained single-target and low-health scaling heroes perform best.
- **Expedition / waves:** AoE damage and crowd control are highly valuable.
- **Defence/garrison:** defensive infantry with shields or healing are preferred.

## How to use this list

Do not chase a higher-tier hero you do not own. Instead, identify the highest-tier heroes in your roster and build a balanced formation around them — see [best heroes](/games/kingshot/guides/best-heroes/) and [best formation](/games/kingshot/guides/best-formation/). We will update this page as rankings change.
